How to read this diagram
  • Left to right: data is produced (simulated building, weather and carbon services), carried by Kafka, processed by independent services, stored in PostgreSQL and shown on the dashboard.
  • Lines: data flowing in the last minute, occasional (e.g. weather every 15 minutes), idle. Thicker lines carry more messages.
  • The loop at the bottom is the feedback path: the AI agent's actions go through the safety check and your approval, then back to the building, which changes the next readings.

Key terms on this page
  • kWh is energy (what you pay for); kW is power, the rate of use at a moment.
  • CO₂e is the carbon emitted to produce that electricity: energy × the grid's carbon intensity (grams of CO₂ per kWh), which comes from Electricity Maps or synthetic data.
  • Baseline is the building's normal fixed schedule: set target temperatures and light levels by time of day. Optimized is what the optimizer chooses: target temperatures and light levels that keep people comfortable while using less energy.
